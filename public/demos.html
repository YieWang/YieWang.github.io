About Me

I am a doctoral researcher advised by Prof. Michael Zhang. My research centers around Multimodal Foundation Models and Efficient Deep Learning.

Specifically, I investigate how to train and compress large vision-language models so that their inference cost scales gracefully with real-world mobile and edge hardware.
